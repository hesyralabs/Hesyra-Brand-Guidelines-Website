01 — BRAND ESSENCE

The end of "Where is my case?"

We help dental clinics move to a fully digital workflow. Dentists upload an intraoral scan; we design, print, cure and polish the restoration and ship it back within 24–72 hours, at a fraction of conventional lab cost.

POSITIONING STATEMENT

For dentists across India who need predictable, lab-grade prosthetics fast, Hesyra Labs is the digital dental lab that fabricates any case on industrial DLP printers and ships it within 48 hours.
